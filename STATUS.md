## 📌 Executive Overview
FahCues is a dynamic, high-voltage comic-book styled web application, e-commerce catalog, and operational cockpit for custom billiard cue craftsman **Travis Kloss** and managing partner **Antonio Moses**. Built with Vite, TypeScript, and modern vanilla CSS, the platform delivers bold pop-art visuals, synthesized Web Audio sound FX, interactive blueprints, and an operational engine that adapts the customer experience across three distinct manufacturing and pricing tiers.

---

## 🎛️ The 3-Tier Workshop Architecture (v0.3.0)
The platform features an operational switcher toggling the entire site between three production realities:
1. **🔥 Street Wraps ($120–$150)**: Immediate hobby and hustle reality. Durable automotive vinyl car wraps and comic decals applied to ~$80 tournament-grade donor blanks. Turnkey in-stock drops with zero client waiting or custom order stress.
2. **🔧 Restomod Studio ($395–$525)**: Send-in re-skinning (Bring Your Own Weapon). Strips clear coat, applies 360° vintage comic decoupage, and cures under 12 coats of high-impact UV clear in 10–14 days.
3. **⚡ Boutique Forge ($1,250–$2,200+)**: 1-of-1 master lathe bespoke builds turned from exotic tonewoods, radial brass joints, and numbered Vault Drops permanently sealed in the Master Registry.
