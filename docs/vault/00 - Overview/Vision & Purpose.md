# Vision & Purpose: FahCues

## The Vision
FahCues is built for pool players, cue collectors, and lovers of underground comic art who refuse to settle for boring, mass-manufactured cues. Founded and crafted by **Travis Kloss**, each cue is an original piece of playable art—turning genuine vintage comic books and rare exotic woods into high-performance weapons on the green felt.

## Core Pillars
1. **Unapologetic Attitude**: High-energy comic styling with vintage pop-art aesthetics and fearless brand voice.
2. **Master Precision**: Beyond aesthetics, each cue is turned on precision lathes with tournament-grade balance, hand-fitted joints, and low-deflection tapers.
3. **Interactive Digital Experience**: The web application mirrors the comic universe—dynamic panels, action bursts, authentic audio feedback, and an interactive cue customizer.
