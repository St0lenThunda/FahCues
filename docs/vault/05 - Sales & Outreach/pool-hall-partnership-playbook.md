## 1. The Idea in One Paragraph

A pool hall buys a **10-cue Showcase Set** (its own house cues re-skinned with genuine vintage comic paper under 12 UV coats and co-branded). On top of that, FahCues places a locked **Demo Rack** of 3-4 cues players can rent, with fees credited toward their own re-skin, and sponsors a **quarterly tournament** whose prize is a re-skin voucher. The set pays the bills; the rack and tournament turn players into buyers.

> **Strategic call**: COUNTER, then GO. Hall pays full price ($395/cue). Never discount; give low-cost extras instead (plaque, QR decals, "Cue of the Quarter" refresh).

## 3. Financial Reality Check (Illustrative)

**Showcase Set**
```text
Hall pays: 10 x $395                          = $3,950   (50% deposit $1,975)
  Bench labor 10 x 5.0 hrs x $50              = -$2,500
  Comics + clear coat + consumables 10 x $75  =   -$750
  Packaging / hand-off 10 x $10               =   -$100
  Ops overhead 10 x $30                       =   -$300
Net on the set:                               =    $300  (~8%)
```
The margin is thin on purpose. The return is advertising plus extras:
- Plaque and QR decals: about $60 total.
- "Cue of the Quarter" refresh: one full-price re-skin per quarter.
- Upsells: matching butt sleeve (+$55 each), armor shield (+$40 each).

**Demo Rack**
- Rental: $10/night or free with 2+ hrs of table time; $50 card hold or ID.
- Rental fees credit toward a re-skin ($25 off the first one).
- Illustration: 4 cues x 20 rentals/month x $10 = $800 gross; 60% hall / 40% FahCues = **$320/month to FahCues**.
- If 3-5% of renters book a re-skin: 2-4 sales/month x $425 = **$850-$1,700/month** (conversion rate is an estimate).

**Tournament Prize**
- Voucher face value: $395. Real cost about $375, only if redeemed.
- A 24-player event at $5/entry adds $120, so exposure is about **$255 per event**.
- Run quarterly, not monthly.

**Capacity warning**: 10 cues = about 50 bench hours = a full month of slots. Phase as **5 cues now, 5 next month**, and cap at 5 slots/month so retail customers aren't crowded out.

## 4. Operating Rules

**Antonio handles**
- One-page agreement, invoices, deposit and balance (balance due **before** batch 2 delivery).
- Screening the 10 cues before shipping: only straight, solid cues; refuse warped/damaged ones. Intake photos plus weight/balance records (Examples 25, 27).
- Rack rules: sign-out sheet, lock, $50 card hold, sanitizing wipes, damage fee schedule.
- Tournament flyers, QR cards, social posts.

**Travis receives**
- A re-skin job sheet per batch of 5 (Example 35). No customer contact, no pricing.

**Key terms for the hall**
1. Hall owns the cues. FahCues owns the artwork and the "FahCues Re-Skin" mark.
2. Hall is responsible for damage beyond normal wear: $40 refinish per ding; $395 re-skin fee for a lost cue.
3. Honest wording only: "re-skinned house cues," never "hand-built."
4. Tournament entry fees and prize rules are the hall's responsibility; FahCues only sponsors the voucher. **Verify Pennsylvania rules and the hall's insurance with an attorney.**
5. 12-month term; right of first refusal on the quarterly "Cue of the Quarter" refresh.
