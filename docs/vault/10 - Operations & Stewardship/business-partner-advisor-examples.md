## Category 1: Custom Commissions & Client Negotiations

### Example 1: The Lowball Price Haggler
**The Situation**: A customer in your DMs loves the Punisher cue listed at $1,250 but offers $800 cash today, claiming "cues are just wood and a pin."  
**Antonio's Prompt**:  
> *"Customer is offering $800 cash for a $1,250 Punisher cue, saying cues are just wood and pins. Give me the breakdown of why we reject this and the exact polite script to stand our ground without burning the bridge."*  
**Advisor Output**:  
1. **Strategic Call**: **HARD REJECTION**. Selling at $800 destroys brand equity and nets less than minimum wage after factoring in $320 in materials and 20+ hours of lathe and curing labor.  
2. **Antonio's Counter Script**:  
```text
"Hey [Name], thanks for the interest! We completely understand having a firm budget. However, our prices reflect 12 hand-buffed coats of UV resin, 18-month cured Canadian tonewoods, and zero-tolerance radial joints turned by hand in Central Pennsylvania. We don’t discount custom builds, as it wouldn't be fair to the collectors who pay full value. If $800 is your hard cap, we occasionally release production break weapons around $950—would you like me to ping you when the next drop goes live?"
```

### Example 2: The High-Pressure "Rush Fee" Dilemma
**The Situation**: A player heading to the BCA National Championships in Las Vegas in 14 days offers a $300 "rush bonus" on top of $1,400 to get a custom cue made.  
**Antonio's Prompt**:  
> *"A tournament player wants a custom build in 14 days and offered a $300 rush fee. Can we do it? If not, how do I explain the chemistry/lathe limits to him?"*  
**Advisor Output**:  
1. **Strategic Call**: **HARD NO**. Automotive-grade urethane over comic decoupage takes 10–14 days just to chemically outgas and harden before high-speed buffing. Rushing will cause the finish to peel or cloud on the plane to Vegas.  
2. **Antonio's Response Script**:  
```text
"We appreciate the tournament urgency and the generous offer! Here is the honest craftsman reality: our ceramic finish takes 14 days just to cure and chemically bond to the wood core before we can even touch it to the buffing wheel. If we rushed it in 14 days, the finish would cloud under pool hall lights and fail on the table. Travis will not let a weapon leave our shop unless it is 100% tournament-ready for a lifetime. Let's build this for your fall season so you have a true heirloom weapon."
```

### Example 3: The Mid-Build Scope Creeper (Wood/Pin Swap)
**The Situation**: A client paid a 50% deposit for a hard maple radial build. 3 weeks in, after Travis already tapered the butt forearm, the client DMs saying they want to switch to fluted purpleheart and a 3/8x10 stainless pin.  
**Antonio's Prompt**:  
> *"Customer wants to change from maple/radial to fluted purpleheart/3/8x10 pin. The forearm is already turned. How do I calculate the change-order fee and break it to the client?"*  
**Advisor Output**:  
1. **Strategic Call**: **CHANGE ORDER APPROVED ONLY WITH FULL REPLACEMENT COST & RESET TIMELINE**. The turned maple piece is now shop scrap or future inventory.  
2. **Financial Math**: Purpleheart timber surcharge ($75) + extra 6 hours fluting labor ($300) + scrapped maple forearm compensation ($120) = **$495 Change Order Fee**. Delivery resets by 4 weeks.  
3. **Antonio's Script**:  
```text
"We can definitely make that purpleheart upgrade happen! Because your maple forearm was already turned and bored for a radial pin, shifting to fluted purpleheart requires turning a fresh exotic blank and cutting new joint threads. The change order fee is $495 (covering the new purpleheart blank, fluting bench time, and the new pin), and pushes completion out by 4 weeks. If you’d like to proceed, I’ll generate the change invoice today!"
```

**The Situation**: A league player sees the $395 re-skin and says, "I can get a vinyl wrap for $60. I'll give you $200 cash."  
**Antonio's Prompt**:  
> *"Guy wants a re-skin for $200 because vinyl wraps are $60. How do I hold the line without sounding defensive?"*  
**Advisor Output**:  
1. **Strategic Call**: **[MODE: RESTOMOD] HOLD THE FLOOR, REFRAME THE COMPARISON.** The price is not for "stickers"; it is for genuine vintage comic paper, hand-laid decoupage, and 12 cured coats that survive chalk, sweat, and years of play. $200 does not cover materials plus bench time.

```text
"Totally fair question! A vinyl wrap is a sticker that peels at the seams in a year. Ours is 100% genuine vintage comic paper laminated into the wood, then sealed under 12 hand-buffed coats of UV clear that become part of the cue. That's why re-skins start at $395. If budget's tight, I can put you on the list for our partial-coverage option (a lower-cost, accent-style re-skin) when we open it. Want me to hold a slot for next month?"
```
3. **Trap to Avoid**: Never discount to $300 "just this once." Offer a *smaller scope*, never a lower price for the same scope.
