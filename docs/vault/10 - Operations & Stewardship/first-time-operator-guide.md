## 💡 The Core Mindset: It's Just One Build a Week
You do not need prior business experience or an MBA to run FahCues. 
At a target throughput of **3 to 4 premium cues per month**, you are managing roughly **one client interaction and one finished product per week**. 

If you follow the 5 core systems below, running this business will take **3 to 5 hours a week**, keeping Travis happy, customers thrilled, and your margins protected.

---

## 1. The 3-Jar Banking Rule (Zero Money Anxiety)
Never mix business money with personal checking. Open one business account with sub-accounts:

1. **Operating Account (Main)**:
   - All 50% deposits and final balances land here.
   - Pay for timbers, pins, Kamui tips, and resin from this card.
2. **Taxes & Tooling Vault (Keep 25% Locked)**:
   - The moment any revenue arrives, immediately transfer:
     - **15% for Taxes** (untouchable until tax filing).
     - **10% for Travis's Lathe Tooling Reserve** (for fresh carbide cutters, sandpaper, and maintenance).
3. **Net Profit Distribution**:
   - The remaining net funds are split 50/50 between Antonio and Travis upon cue completion.

---

## 2. The 5-Card Visual Pipeline (No Mental Overload)
Manage every cue on a simple 5-column board (Trello, Notion, or a physical clipboard):

```text
[ 1. INQUIRY ] ──► [ 2. DEPOSIT PAID ] ──► [ 3. IN LATHE ] ──► [ 4. CURING ] ──► [ 5. SHIPPED ]
 Client asks for     50% received            Travis turning     Chemical cure      Balance cleared
 custom quote        Specs locked            timber             (14 days min)      Insured track #
```
**Golden Cap**: Never allow more than 3 to 4 active cues in columns 2 through 4 simultaneously. If the shop is full, book new inquiries for the following month.

---

## 3. The 24-Hour Breathing Room Rule
Never quote prices or confirm custom specs on the spot in real time:
- Always use the **24-Hour Buffer Script**:
  > *"Travis and I love this concept. Let me check our shop lathe calendar and wood inventory tonight, and I will have a formal specification sheet and quote over to you tomorrow afternoon."*
- Open your editor, run `/biz-advisor`, verify the math, and return with a poised, professional proposal.

---

## 4. The Sunday 30-Minute Rhythm
Spend exactly 30 minutes every Sunday morning running the business:
- **10 Mins (Cash Check)**: Log any cleared deposits, reimburse material expenses.
- **10 Mins (Travis Alignment)**: Brief text to Travis: *"Hey brother, how are cues #003 and #004 curing? Need any tips or pins staged for this week?"*
- **10 Mins (Proactive Client Ping)**: Send a 1-sentence update to active buyers before they have to ask:
  > *"Quick update: your forearm passed final balance and acoustic check this week. We’re moving to decoupage next. Hope you have a great week!"*

---

## 5. What To Do When Things Go Wrong
- **Client asks for a refund**: Check your 50% non-refundable deposit terms. Run `/biz-advisor` for the de-escalation script.
- **Travis finds a defect**: Enforce the *Craftsman Dignity* policy. Tell the client that our surgical 10x inspection caught an imperfection, and we are recuring it to ensure lifetime quality.
- **You feel overwhelmed**: Cap new orders for 30 days. Let the lathe catch up. Quality and sane work-life balance always beat volume.
