## Executive Summary: The "Artisan + Operator" Model
FahCues represents an extraordinary intersection of high-precision billiard cue craftsmanship and iconic comic pop-art culture. 

The primary constraint on the business today is the **Artisan's Bottleneck**: the friction of client haggling, pricing uncertainty, administrative overhead, tech maintenance, and invoicing distracts Travis Kloss from his core gift on the lathe.

**The Solution**: Establish a two-person partnership where:
- **Travis Kloss** focuses 100% on wood lathe turning, stabilization, comic decoupage, precision joint fitting, and acoustic hit-testing.
- **Antonio Moses** runs 100% of the commercial apparatus: client qualification, pricing enforcement, upfront deposit collection, website and digital registry infrastructure, insured shipping, and brand storytelling.

## 2. Monthly Economics & Financial Waterfall (4-Cue Baseline)

Based on a steady, sustainable throughput of **4 cues per month** (2 Vault collection drops + 1 custom commission + 1 heavy break cue):

### Monthly Projected Revenue: $5,300.00
- 2 Vault Themed Cues: $1,350 × 2 = $2,700.00
- 1 Custom Bespoke Commission: $1,550.00
- 1 Heavy Break Weapon (*The Smokin' 8*): $1,050.00

### The 4-Stage Distribution Waterfall:
```text
Gross Revenue: $5,300.00
  ├── 1. Direct COGS Reimbursement:        -$1,100.00 (Hard maple, exotic burls, pins, tips, resin)
  ├── 2. Operating & Tax Reserve (15%):      -$630.00 (Locked in business checking for taxes)
  ├── 3. Shop Tooling & Consumables (10%):   -$420.00 (Travis lathe cutters, sandpaper, shop supplies)
  └── 4. Net Distributable Profit:          $3,150.00
          ├── Travis Kloss (Craft Share - 50%):   $1,575.00
          └── Antonio Moses (Ops Share - 50%):    $1,575.00
```

### Direct Value to Travis:
- **Take-Home Cash Profit**: **$1,575.00/month**
- **Dedicated Shop Tooling Reserve**: **$420.00/month** (Zero out-of-pocket spending on lathe maintenance)
- **Effective Net Realization**: **>$80–$100 per bench hour** with zero unbilled administrative time.

## 3. The Three Craftsman Guarantees

1. **Veto Power on Craft**: Travis holds absolute authority on structural integrity. If a client requests a technically unsound specification, Travis has complete authority to reject or modify the build.
2. **The "No Burnout" Throughput Cap**: Antonio will never book more than 4 cues in a calendar month without Travis's prior written consent.
3. **The Customer Shield**: Travis will never have to speak with difficult or angry customers. Antonio manages all customer communication, delays, and expectations.

---

## 4. The 90-Day Proof of Concept (Zero-Risk Trial)

Instead of formalizing an immediate long-term legal commitment, Antonio proposes a **3-Cue Pilot**:
1. Travis turns a 3-cue curated collection of his choice ("The Vault Drop").
2. Antonio handles staging of materials, photography, web showcase, marketing, and sales at full luxury retail pricing ($1,250–$1,650).
3. Profits are split per the waterfall formula above.
4. After 90 days, both partners review results and decide whether to formalize the long-term partnership.
